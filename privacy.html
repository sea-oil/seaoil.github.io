CUSTOMER EXPERIENCES

How we handle reviews.

We value honest experiences and respect your privacy.

Information collected

When you submit a C-OIL review, we collect the rating, comment, optional name, and submission date. A security challenge checks for automated submissions; a limited technical identifier derived from your network address is used to prevent repeated spam.

What becomes public

If approved, your rating, comment, displayed name (or “Anonymous customer”), and date appear on the C-OIL website. Please do not include phone numbers, addresses, medical details, or other personal information in your review.

How reviews are managed

The C-OIL owner can approve, hide, reject, or delete reviews. Honest critical feedback is welcome. No payment or purchase is required to submit a review, and we do not claim reviews are from verified purchasers.

Storage and service providers

Reviews are stored in Supabase. Cloudflare Turnstile checks whether the submission is automated. Technical anti-spam information is used only to protect the form. Reviews may remain stored until they are deleted, including through a request that can be reasonably verified.

Requests and questions

If you want to request removal of a review or ask about your information, contact imgathuri@gmail.com or call +254727732832. Please describe the review without sending sensitive identity documents.
